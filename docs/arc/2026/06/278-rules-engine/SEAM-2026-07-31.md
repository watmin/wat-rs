## The lesson that still governs every instrument here

An instrument certifies exactly the span it times. The grid times `fire-rules` **only** — on
node-share that was 1.4% of the process, and on `accum` today it is 22% while insertion is 74%. So a
grid verdict is a statement about the fire, never about the system. **Before trusting any instrument:
what fraction of the work does the timed span cover, and is the case it runs the expensive one?**

`Vector` (cheap constant) instead of a `PersistentVector` (trie rebuild). Fix the SUBJECT, never the
wall. This is why today's gate counts examinations instead of nanoseconds.
