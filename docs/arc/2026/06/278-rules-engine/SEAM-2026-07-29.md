## The lessons this session paid for

**★ A BRIEF'S TYPE IS A CLAIM AND OWES EVIDENCE.** I specified
`:Value [value <- :wat::core::Value]` — the universal top, where UP is free and DOWN is CHECKED —
for a payload whose entire purpose is to be consumed. The rider built exactly that, hit the wall my
